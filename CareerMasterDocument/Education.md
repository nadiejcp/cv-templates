# Academic Education (Master Repository)

> **Template Purpose:**  
> This file is a **Master Academic Education Repository**. It stores your complete academic history, including graduate degrees, undergraduate programs, international exchanges, research theses, honors, and comprehensive coursework.  
> An AI agent will read this file to extract your credentials, prioritize relevant coursework based on the specific job description's keywords, and position your educational background optimally on your CV.

---

## 💡 How to Document Education for AI Parsing

> [!TIP]
> **Key Elements to Include:**  
> 1. **Official Degree & Major:** State the full formal degree title (e.g., *Master of Science in Data Science*, *Bachelor of Science in Engineering*).
> 2. **Institution & Location:** University name, campus, city, and country.
> 3. **Dates / Status:** Graduation month/year, or *"In Progress / Expected [Date]"* (or leave open as `[Dates: ]`).
> 4. **GPA & Honors (Optional):** Include if $\ge$ 3.5/4.0, Latin honors (*Cum Laude*), or top percentile rank.
> 5. **Categorized Coursework:** Group coursework by subject area so the AI can pull only the courses that match a specific job description.
> 6. **Thesis / Capstone Project:** Title, methodology, tools, and real-world results.

## 🤖 Instructions for AI CV-Crafting Agent

> 1. **Placement Strategy:**
>    - **For Mid-to-Senior Professional CVs (3+ years experience):** Place the **Education** section near the bottom (after Experience and Projects). Keep each degree to 2–3 compact lines.
>    - **For Academic / Research / Graduate Program Applications:** Place the **Education** section near the top (right after the Summary). Expand thesis details and academic honors.
> 2. **Coursework Filtering Rule:**
>    - Do **not** list all coursework on a tailored CV. Dynamically select **4 to 6 courses** that directly match the keywords of the target job description (e.g., pick *Database Architecture* for backend roles, *Machine Learning* for AI roles).
> 3. **Formatting Standard:**
>    `**[Degree Title]** | [Institution Name] — [Location] ([Graduation Year or Expected Date])`  
>    `*Relevant Coursework:* [Selected 4–6 relevant courses matching the target job]`  
>    *(Include Thesis/Capstone line only if directly relevant to the target role).*
