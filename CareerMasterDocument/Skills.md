# Core Skills & Competencies (Master Repository)

> **Template Purpose:**  
> This file is a **Master Functional Skills Inventory**. Unlike `Technologies.md` (which lists raw tools and programming languages), this document details your **engineering capabilities, system design methodologies, architectural proficiencies, and leadership competencies**.  
> An AI agent will draw from these functional pillars to structure your CV's "Core Competencies" section and craft achievement-oriented bullet points that prove not just that you know a tool, but *how* you apply it to deliver business value.

---

## 💡 How to Document Skills for AI Parsing

> [!TIP]
> **Functional Skill Structure:**  
> **[Competency Category]:**  
> - **Core Capabilities:** Functional actions (e.g., *Architecting*, *Designing*, *Optimizing*, *Orchestrating*).  
> - **Methodologies & Patterns:** Design patterns and engineering principles applied (e.g., *3NF Normalization*, *ACID*, *Microservices*, *Clean Architecture*).  
> - **Target Role Tags:** Which job profiles this competency supports (e.g., `[Backend | AI/ML | DevOps | Leadership]`).
