# Certifications & Specialized Training (Master Repository)

> **Template Purpose:**  
> This file is a **Master Certifications & Continuous Learning Repository**. It stores an exhaustive, uncapped record of all your professional certifications, vendor credentials (AWS, GCP, Azure, CKA), university micro-credentials (edX, Coursera, Harvard), intensive bootcamps, and specialized technical workshops.  
> An AI agent will filter this list to extract only the 2–4 most relevant credentials and technical competencies that match the requirements and ATS keywords of a specific target role.

## 💡 How to Log Certifications for AI Parsing

> [!TIP]
> **Key Information to Capture for Each Certification:**  
> 1. **Full Official Credential Name:** Include the issuing vendor (e.g., *AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate*).
> 2. **Issuing Organization & Platform:** (e.g., *Amazon Web Services*, *Harvard Online / edX*, *DeepLearning.AI*).
> 3. **Dates & Credential Verification:** Issue date, expiration (if applicable), and Credential ID / Verification URL.
> 4. **Duration / Rigor:** Total hours or months (e.g., *40 hours*, *3-month specialization*).
> 5. **Core Competencies & Hands-On Projects:** Specific libraries, architectures, and tools mastered.
> 6. **Target Role Tags:** Labels like `[AI/ML]`, `[Cloud/DevOps]`, `[Data Engineering]`, `[Software Architecture]`.

## 🤖 Instructions for AI CV-Crafting Agent

> - **Selective Inclusion Rule:** Do not include every certification on a 1- or 2-page CV. Dynamically select the **top 2 to 4 certifications** that directly reinforce the primary job requirements.
> - **ATS Keyword Matching:** Match exact keywords from the job description (e.g., if the posting asks for *"PostgreSQL and Schema Optimization"*, highlight the *Harvard CS50 Databases* certification).
> - **Standardized Formatting:** Present selected certifications on the CV cleanly:  
>   `**[Certification Title]** – [Issuing Organization] ([Year])`  
>   *(Optionally add a 1-line sub-bullet with key tools or verification link if space permits).*
