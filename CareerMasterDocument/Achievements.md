## 💡 How to Write High-Impact Achievements for AI Parsing

> [!TIP]
> **Formula for Standout Achievements:**  
> **[Award / Distinction Name] + [Issuing Organization & Date] + [Selectivity / Scope Metric] + [Action / Project Description] + [Relevance Tags]**  
> 
> - **Quantify Selectivity:** Mention acceptance rates, ranking, dollar amounts, or candidate pool sizes (e.g., *"Selected 1st out of 80 teams"*, *"Awarded $15,000 competitive grant with <5% acceptance"*).
> - **Highlight Impact & Tech:** Note the methodology, tool, or breakthrough that led to the achievement.
> - **Tag by Role/Domain:** Label each achievement so the AI knows when to surface it (e.g., `[Target: AI/ML]`, `[Target: Leadership]`, `[Target: Research]`).
